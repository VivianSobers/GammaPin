# Trading calendar for the build window

Nifty weekly options expire on **Tuesday**, Sensex on **Thursday** (NSE/BSE change from September 2025).
When expiry day is a holiday, expiry moves to the **previous trading day**.

## NSE holidays, October to December 2026

Compiled from broker-published copies of the NSE list. Check the official NSE circular before relying on it, especially for November, where sources disagree.

| Date | Day | Holiday | Effect on Nifty expiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Oct 2026 | Fri | Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti | None |
| 20 Oct 2026 | **Tue** | Dussehra | Expiry moves to **Mon 19 Oct** |
| 8 Nov 2026 | Sun | Diwali Laxmi Pujan (Muhurat trading session, timings by circular) | None |
| 9 or 10 Nov 2026 | Mon or **Tue** | Diwali Balipratipada (sources differ) | If Tue 10 Nov: expiry moves to Mon 9 Nov |
| 24 Nov 2026 | **Tue** | Guru Nanak Jayanti | Expiry moves to **Mon 23 Nov** |
| 25 Dec 2026 | Fri | Christmas | None |

## What this means for the 14-day plan

| Start (Wed) | Day 7 expiry | Day 14 expiry | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 Oct 2026 | Mon 19 Oct (day 6, Dussehra shift) | Tue 27 Oct | Expiry 1 arrives a day early: V4 must be ready by day 6 |
| **21 Oct 2026** | Tue 27 Oct | Tue 3 Nov | Clean, no shifts. Recommended |
| 28 Oct 2026 | Tue 3 Nov | Tue 10 Nov or Mon 9 Nov | Day 14 depends on the Balipratipada date |
